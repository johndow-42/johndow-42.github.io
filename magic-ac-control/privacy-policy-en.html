Privacy Policy Magic AC Control

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Summary: The app controls your air conditioners through the manufacturer's cloud. Your login details go only to that cloud. Your ongoing position never leaves the phone. In shared storage (the default), the schedule, the names of the people, their status (home or away), the centre of the "Home" area and a short log are kept in Google Firebase. No ads, no tracking, no sharing for other purposes.

Magic AC Control is an unofficial app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or reviewed by Toshiba or Carrier.

Controller

The developer of the app (johndow42), contact: johndow42ai@gmail.com.

Login details for the air conditioner cloud

To control your units you sign in with the account you also use in the manufacturer's app (Toshiba Home AC Control). User name and password are stored encrypted on the device (Android Keystore) and are sent only to the manufacturer's servers (mobileapi.toshibahomeaccontrols.com and the related messaging service). Commands go there as well, and device states come from there. The manufacturer's privacy policy also applies to this processing. We never receive your login details.

Location

If you turn on "Geofence auf diesem Handy" (geofence on this phone) in the settings, the app detects when you enter or leave the "Home" area, even when it is closed. This requires background location. Detection is done by Google Play services on your phone (geofencing). The app neither stores nor transmits your ongoing position. Only the result is transmitted: home or away. You set the centre and radius of the "Home" area yourself, optionally with a button that takes your location at that moment. Only that one position is then stored as the centre. Without these features the app does not use location.

Storage: local or shared

In the settings you choose where the schedule, people and the "Home" area are kept. The default is "Shared"; you can change it at any time.

  • Local: on this device only. None of it goes to Firebase.
  • Shared: in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), so several phones in a household see the same data. Stored are the schedule and the states per room including the device identifiers, the names of the people you create, their status with the time of the last change, the centre and radius of the "Home" area and a log of the last 50 events (for example "Anna arrived" with the time, or "Living room: away, off"). The household is stored under an identifier derived via SHA-256 from your account with the manufacturer. Name, e-mail address and password are not stored there. Every installation signs in to Firebase anonymously.
  • Own Firebase project: like "Shared", but in a project you run yourself. You are then responsible for that data.

Regardless of storage: weekly programs you send to your units go through the manufacturer's cloud and are also stored there.

Everyone using the same manufacturer account sees the same household. You create the people yourself; if you use real names, please let those people know. A nickname is enough.

Purpose and legal basis

Data is processed only to provide the features you use: controlling air conditioners, running schedules and switching rooms by presence (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Background location is used only after you agree in the app and grant the permission in the system (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time by turning off the geofence switch or revoking the location permission in the Android settings.

Recipients

  • The manufacturer of the air conditioner cloud (login details, commands, weekly programs, device states).
  • Google (Firebase, only with shared storage, and Google Play services for geofencing). Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Beyond that, no data is shared or sold.

Retention and deletion

Local data and login details are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in the Android settings. Data in shared storage is kept until it is deleted. You can delete people in the app; the "Home" area can only be reset there. Older log entries with a name drop out after 50 newer events. To have the whole household deleted, send an e-mail to the address above; since we store no account details, we will work out with you how to identify your household, for example by the names of the people you created.

No ads, no tracking

The app contains no ads, no analytics or tracking libraries and sends no crash reports to third parties.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Use the address above for any request.
